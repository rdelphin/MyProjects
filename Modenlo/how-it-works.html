Image Requirements & Tips

Get the best results from your photos

📸

Resolution

For best results, use high-resolution images (at least 300 DPI). Our system will check your image quality and alert you if adjustments are needed.

📐

File Format

We accept JPG, PNG, and HEIC files. Most photos from smartphones and digital cameras work perfectly.

🎨

Color & Lighting

Bright, well-lit photos with good contrast produce the most stunning metal prints. Avoid overly dark or washed-out images.

✂️

Cropping

Make sure important subjects aren't too close to edges. Our preview tool lets you adjust positioning before ordering.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does production take?

Most orders are produced and shipped within 3-5 business days. You'll receive tracking information once your order ships.

Can I see a proof before production?

Our preview tool shows you exactly how your print will look. What you see in the preview is what you'll receive!

What if I'm not satisfied with my print?

We offer a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not completely happy with your metal print, contact us and we'll make it right.

Are the mounts included?

Yes! Your selected mount option is included in the price. All prints come ready to hang or display right out of the box.

Can I order custom sizes?

Absolutely! While we offer popular sizes, we also provide custom sizing options to fit your exact specifications.

How do I care for my metal print?

Metal prints are easy to maintain. Simply wipe with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive materials.

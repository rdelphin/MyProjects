Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. Expedited options are available at checkout. Free shipping on all orders over $50.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with your print, contact us for a full refund or replacement.

Do you offer custom sizes?

Yes! While we have popular sizes available, we also offer custom sizing. Contact us with your specific dimensions for a quote.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this to monitor your shipment's progress.

What file formats do you accept?

We accept JPG, PNG, and HEIC files. For best results, upload high-resolution images (at least 300 DPI).

Do you offer bulk/wholesale pricing?

Yes! We have special pricing for bulk orders and wholesale customers. Use the contact form above with subject "Wholesale Inquiry".
